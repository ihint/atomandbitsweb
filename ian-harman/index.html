About Ian

I’m Ian.
I like a hard problem
and a good question.

I’m a founder and product and technology leader based in Chattanooga. Through Atom & Bits, I work with founders and teams to bring the business, product, and technology into one view.

What I bring

A founder’s experience and an operator’s responsibility.

I’ve co-founded a company, won customers, led an acquisition, built teams, and worked across enterprise sales, software, and operations.

My approach

Joy, curiosity,
and a shared understanding.

I ask questions to gather information, understand other perspectives, and earn buy-in. I want us to agree on the problem and what the deliverable will actually be.

My work connects strategy and customer needs to product choices, technical decisions, and delivery. The question is where a change can create the most value for the business.

I’ve spent more than 15 years in product and technology leadership. Healthcare is my deepest area of experience; my advisory work spans manufacturing and health tech.

Today, I’m also building Claims Native and internal agent workspaces to put that operating approach into software.
