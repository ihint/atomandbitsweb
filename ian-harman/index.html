I’m Ian Harman.

For 15 years I’ve worked on the systems people depend on but rarely see: how a nurse reaches a doctor at 2 a.m., how a member finds their benefits, how a therapist gets matched and paid. The products I’ve helped build have reached millions of people across the country, from health systems in Chicago, Orlando and Houston to BlueCross members across Tennessee.

I live in Chattanooga, Tennessee, with my wife, our three sons, three dogs and ten chickens. Home is loud, busy and full, and I wouldn’t have it any other way.

When I’m not working, you’ll find me on a ski slope every chance I get, in the stands or on the couch for Volunteer football, or in the woodshop with a cold brew. Lately I’m refining my taste in wine, learning the finer points of chess, and teaching myself to carve a spoon while I work my way through Dostoevsky.

I also love cooking a good meal for the people I love, and it might be the closest thing to how I build software and businesses. The recipe might call for one portion, but as the chef I know we need to double it to make it hit just right. It’s also one of the best places I know to be creative.

From hospital floors to fintech.

  • PerfectServe · I started in hospital implementations as the company’s first product manager, then provided product leadership as it grew from $2M to $40M ARR.
  • BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee · I was a founding member of the digital team, leading products for 3.5M members, brokers and employers, with 22 direct reports and 130+ people across teams.
  • One to One Health · I built product and engineering from scratch, served as Chief Privacy Officer, and launched the company’s first AI product.
  • TheraMatch and TPN.health · I co-founded TheraMatch, built the product and technology behind its clinical matching algorithm, and signed customers before raising money. I partnered with Kolby on its sale to TPN.health. After TPN raised $12M, we scaled it to 1M members in under a year, and I led the claims and payments platform behind 150,000+ clinicians. I was VP, Provider Networks & Technology at TPN.health from Feb 2025 to Aug 2026.
  • Now · Claims Native for independent practices, Corridor Robotics, which puts AI to work in Chattanooga businesses, and advisory work through Atom & Bits.

Curiosity, care, and a willingness to lead.

I read a lot before I say much, and then I ask questions. At TPN, asking what payer invoicing was meant to achieve led us to an internal claims filing and revenue capture platform. Wanting to know what happened after a TheraMatch recommendation led us to check whether each person was still with their first therapist a month later. Moments like those, when one question changes what a team decides to build, are my favorite part.

Chattanooga keeps that care close to home. Our children are growing up here, and I serve on the board of The Mission Chattanooga. I want the teams I work with to feel the same kindness: room to disagree, to learn, and to contribute to something useful.

The projects I’m proudest of all ended the same way: a nurse, a member or a therapist using what we built every day and never having to think about it.
