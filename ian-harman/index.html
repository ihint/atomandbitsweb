About Ian

I’m Ian Harman.

I live in Chattanooga, Tennessee, with my wife and family. We have three children, three dogs, and ten chickens. We’re grateful to live and work in this community.

Throughout my career, I’ve used technology, both hardware and software, to support people on the front lines doing some of the hardest jobs. I care about making their lives better, and about what that makes possible for the clients, patients, and users who depend on them.

How I got here

The business continues after the product ships.

My healthcare career began in hospital implementations at PerfectServe. Working where a product had to become part of someone’s day taught me to pay attention to what happened after the sale. That question has stayed with me through enterprise leadership, building a care business, and founding a company of my own.

At BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee, my work grew from member mobile products into a wider digital portfolio. I built and coached teams, worked across business and technology groups, and helped make services easier for members to use.

At One to One Health, I built the product and engineering function and worked on the relationship between growth and the team’s ability to deliver care. Later, I co-founded TheraMatch, developed the product and early clinical framework, won physician customers, and led its acquisition and integration with TPN.health.

Those experiences gave me different responsibilities. I have had to consider what a customer would buy, what a team could deliver, and what the business would need once people began using the product. Through Atom & Bits, I work with founders and teams to connect the business they want to build with what it takes to run it. My advisory work spans manufacturing and health tech.

Working together

Curiosity, care, and a willingness to lead.

I do a lot of research before I formulate a response, and I enjoy the curiosity a good conversation can open up. I want to hear what people have noticed, understand the words we are using, and work toward an answer we can explain to one another.

Kindness matters to me. So do thoughtful disagreement and the willingness to learn. There is real joy in working with people who care about the opportunity to make something useful, and who give each other room to contribute.

I welcome conversations about founder, operating, advisory, and portfolio-company work.
