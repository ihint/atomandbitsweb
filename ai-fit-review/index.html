Product & AI assessment

One decision.
Two weeks.
A next step we agree on.

Bring one product or workflow decision. I’ll work with your team to understand the problem, compare the options, and decide what deserves to be built.

The AI Fit Review

Build, buy, change the process, or stop.

The recommendation follows the work, the economics, and the evidence. AI earns its place when it improves the result.

When it helps

Your team needs a decision
it can act on.

01

A product choice

You need to choose which customer problem to solve, what the first product should do, or whether an opportunity is worth pursuing.

02

An AI opportunity

A repeated task looks suitable for automation. You need to understand the inputs, exceptions, human decisions, and value before funding a pilot.

03

An operating obstacle

People move the same information through inboxes, documents, portals, or spreadsheets. You need to identify what should change first.

What you leave with

The problem, the recommendation,
and the agreed deliverable.

01

A shared view of the work

The workflow, people, systems, rules, delays, and current cost. We reflect it back together so missing context can change the plan.

02

A decision with reasons

The options, expected value, risks, and unknowns. Where AI fits, where fixed rules fit, and where the process needs attention.

03

A scoped next step

The deliverable, owner, system boundaries, completion measures, and a plan for the next 90 days.

How we begin

A short fit conversation,
then a fixed scope.

What I need from you

One named decision, an available workflow owner, and people who can explain the work. We agree on access and use de-identified examples for healthcare work.

The two-week schedule begins at kickoff once the scope, access, and participants are ready.

What the assessment covers

The decision and plan. Production software, implementation, and ongoing leadership have their own scope.

We agree on the fixed fee before kickoff. You can use the plan with your team, another partner, or Atom & Bits.
