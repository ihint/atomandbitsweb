Support

Support for Atom & Bits products and services.

Use this page for product support, mobile app questions, purchase restoration help, or business inquiries related to Atom & Bits.

Consulting and advisory inquiries

For healthcare product, technology, AI workflow, provider-network, or platform advisory conversations, email Ian directly with the context and desired outcome.

Mobile app support

If you need help with an Atom & Bits mobile app, include the app name, device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Purchase help

  • Use the app's Restore Purchases flow to restore eligible purchases for the same Apple ID.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • For billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Read the Atom & Bits privacy policy at privacy-policy.html.

Contact

Send the shortest useful description of the issue or opportunity, plus any screenshots or details that would help reproduce the problem.
