Atom & Bits / TheraMatch

TheraMatch

Lowering the barrier to entry.

Ian Harman · Co-Founder · August 2024–April 2025

The right therapist can change a life. I co-founded TheraMatch to help each person seeking care find a therapist they might feel able to work with, matched on clinical fit and their own preferences. After TPN.health acquired the TheraMatch technology, TheraMatch and TPN Match reached $1M+ ARR and 1M members in under 12 months.

Making care easier to reach

Make the first connection easier.

When someone decides to seek help, I want their next step to be a person they can begin with. TheraMatch set out to give them enough information to make that useful first connection.

I built the product, technology, and strategy, including the initial matching algorithm and clinical delivery framework for our early clients. Our matching logic brought together clinical frameworks, patient preferences, and provider information so each recommendation could weigh more than location or availability.

Each match had people on every side of it. Physician practices wanted a referral path they could use. Therapists chose to take part. The person seeking care wanted a connection they could begin with. Winning physician customers, welcoming therapists, and helping each person make a useful connection were all part of building TheraMatch.

Product decisions

Follow the connection beyond the recommendation.

I chose to build clinical fit into the product itself, using more than 20 psychographic measures and clinical frameworks. I built the app with Elixir, Phoenix, LiveView, and Ecto, including the intake, matching logic, data model, and operating tools.

I wanted to know what happened after a recommendation. Was the person still with their first therapist a month later? We measured exactly that at the one-month follow-up, and it let us learn about the connection itself, beyond a click or an introduction.

A matching score could help inform a choice. It could not guarantee a therapeutic relationship or a clinical outcome. That relationship belongs to the person and their therapist, and we built TheraMatch to help them begin it.

Customers and acquisition

From 12 physician clients to TPN Match.

Physician practices were our first customers. We won 12 physician clients across three practices, and about 40 providers took part. About 300 matches formed the basis of the reported 98% one-month first-match retention result. TheraMatch reached about $12,000 in monthly recurring revenue before acquisition.

Typical contract value was $4,000–$5,000 from September 2024 to January 2025. We shaped terms physician customers would accept and worked to make TheraMatch useful enough to become part of their day.

TPN.health announced its acquisition of All Counseling and the TheraMatch technology on February 27, 2025. I led the acquisition and the integration. Our matching product joined a broader provider-access company, which opened new ways to serve people through networks, navigation, claims, and payment.

TheraMatch and TPN Match reached $1M+ ARR and 1M members in under 12 months. A matching product that began with 12 physician clients now reached far more people, and that reach opened the claims and payment questions I describe in the TPN Match story.
