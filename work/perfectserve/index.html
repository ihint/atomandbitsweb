Atom & Bits / PerfectServe

PerfectServe

New providers ready in under an hour.

Ian Harman · Implementation, Product & Product Marketing Leadership · May 2011–April 2016

At PerfectServe, I worked on how a nurse reaches a doctor at 2 a.m. My roles spanned implementation, product management and product marketing as the company grew from $2M to $40M ARR. One change says the most about those years: we cut provider onboarding from five to seven days to under one hour, so a new provider could start using PerfectServe within the hour instead of waiting most of a week.

What I carry forward

A customer’s first hour matters as much as the sale.

I want founders to see a detail like that onboarding change next to the sales numbers. It answers a question I like to ask: what does each new customer need from you before they can benefit from what they bought?

The answer can point to the product, to how you introduce it or to the support around it, and each calls for a different investment. Working out which comes first is a puzzle I enjoy.
