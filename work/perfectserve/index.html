PerfectServe

Enterprise scaling and adoption.

Ian Harman · Implementation, Product & Product Marketing Leadership · May 2011–April 2016

An enterprise sale is the start of a relationship the company has to sustain. The customer needs to get started, bring people into the product, and keep using it. At PerfectServe, I worked across implementation, product management, and product marketing as the company grew from $2M to $40M ARR, connecting those needs with enterprise rollout, adoption, and new-market sales.

The opportunity

Make enterprise growth easier to support.

01

Win the right business

I built a new-market sales approach and connected customer and buyer needs to the product.

02

Deliver the rollout

I led or co-led 10 health-system implementations tied to more than 10% ARR growth.

03

Shorten the path to first use

I helped build product and admin systems and cut provider onboarding from five to seven days to under one hour.

What I carry forward

Growth depends on what happens after the sale.

Reducing provider onboarding from five to seven days to under one hour changed how quickly people could begin using the product. That is the kind of detail I want a founder to see alongside the sales numbers: what does each new customer need from the company before they can benefit from what they bought?

The answer can change where we invest. It may point to the product, the way we introduce it, or the support it requires. I bring that understanding of enterprise adoption to my engagements through Atom & Bits.

About these results

These are my reported career results. The $2M to $40M figure describes company ARR growth to which I contributed. The new-market revenue and implementation measures describe separate work and should not be added together.
