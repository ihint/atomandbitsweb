One to One Health

From 22 to 17.5 minutes
of charting.

Ian Harman · VP, Product & Engineering; Chief Privacy Officer · October 2023–June 2024

I identified the charting problem and designed and developed the initial AI infrastructure for provider SOAP-note creation. The workflow prepared a draft for provider review.

The result

Less charting work, with provider review.

Charting time fell from 22 to 17.5 minutes across all encounters while maintaining or improving both clinician and patient satisfaction.

The problem

Every encounter
needed a complete note.

The repeated work

Clinicians needed to turn patient and provider information into a structured SOAP note. Capturing and writing the note added work to each encounter.

The decision that mattered

The system could prepare the draft. The provider needed to keep responsibility for clinical accuracy and the final note.

What I built

AI preparation.
Clinical review.

01

Capture the information

Patient and provider input supplied the facts needed for the encounter note.

02

Prepare the draft

The system organized those facts into Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan sections.

03

Review and chart

The provider reviewed, corrected, and approved the note before it entered the chart.

The broader role

Build the product
and the team around it.

Product and engineering

I identified the problem, developed the initial AI infrastructure, and hired the founding engineers.

Operating responsibility

As Chief Privacy Officer and product and engineering leader, I also led privacy work and negotiated a 17% reduction in IT spending.

About these results

These are my reported results from the work. The charting measure covers all encounters; it is not a measure of patient contact time. Clinician and patient satisfaction were maintained or improved. The work reflects the contributions of the clinical and engineering teams.

What I carry forward

Prepare useful work.
Keep the human decision clear.

That choice now informs the agent workflows I’m building at Atom & Bits.
