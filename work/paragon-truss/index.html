Paragon Truss

Paragon’s first enterprise contract.

Ian Harman · Atom & Bits · Product & Technology Advisor

I advised Paragon on product direction and its move into AI, and led its first enterprise sale. I brought product discovery, contract negotiations and the client relationship together around a clear business case and a delivery commitment the team could meet.

Closing Paragon’s first enterprise sale

Put what the customer valued into the contract.

I led the deal, contracts and client conversations for a contract valued at $500,000 a year. As an advisor, I also worked on product discovery and an AI product pivot.

I stayed directly involved with the customer through the close. Knowing the product well mattered, because the terms had to describe something the buyer valued and Paragon could commit to delivering.

A customer wants to know what they are buying and why it matters to them. A company selling it wants a clear account of what it is agreeing to deliver. I enjoy the moment both sides read the same terms and see the same thing.
