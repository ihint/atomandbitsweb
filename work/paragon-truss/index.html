Atom & Bits / Paragon Truss

Paragon Truss

Winning the first enterprise customer.

Ian Harman · Atom & Bits · Product & Technology Advisor

A first enterprise deal asks a company to turn the promise of its product into an agreement a customer can act on. The product, the commercial terms, and the relationship all need attention.

The engagement

Product understanding in the customer conversation.

At Paragon Truss, I led the deal, contracts, and client interaction for the company’s first enterprise contract, valued at $500,000 annually. My advisory work also covered product discovery and an AI product pivot.

The commercial responsibility was part of my work with the company. I was directly involved in the customer relationship and in taking the agreement through to a close. That work drew on both product understanding and enterprise business development.

A prospective customer needs to understand what they are buying and why it matters to their business. The company selling it needs a clear account of what it is agreeing to deliver. I enjoy working where those two understandings have to come together.

The result

A $500,000 annual enterprise contract.

The result was Paragon Truss’s first enterprise contract, valued at $500,000 per year. I led the deal and contract work with the client.

This is part of the breadth I bring through Atom & Bits. My deepest experience is in healthcare, and my advisory work also includes manufacturing. The responsibility is to understand the particular business well enough to make a useful product and commercial decision.

About this account and these results

The role and contract result are my reported experience. The $500,000 figure is annual contract value, not a statement of recognized or collected revenue. The account does not attribute all company work to me.
