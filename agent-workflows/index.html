Agent workflow pilot

Let’s take one task off your team’s plate and see what happens.

Most teams have a task that eats hours every week: chasing a document, re-entering data, checking the same thing twice. A pilot puts an AI agent on that one task, with a person approving the steps that matter.

What you get

A working agent on one real workflow, a measure of the time it saved, and a clear yes or no on whether to expand it.
