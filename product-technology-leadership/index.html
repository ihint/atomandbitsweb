Product & technology leadership

Decide what comes next. See it through.

Growing a company can ask more of its founder than one person can keep taking on. Product choices affect the sales conversation. Technical decisions affect what the team can promise. A new customer changes what you need to be able to deliver.

I work with founders, operating teams, and portfolio companies when those decisions need experienced leadership. We agree on what I will own, give the team a direction it can work from, and stay close to what happens as those choices take effect.

Relevant experience

Build around what customers value.

At TheraMatch, I owned product, technology, and strategy, developed the early matching and clinical framework, and led the company through acquisition and integration. At Paragon Truss, I led the deal, contracts, and client interaction for its first enterprise contract, valued at $500,000 annually.

Those are different kinds of work, but each requires an understanding of customers, costs, and delivery that holds up in practice. A product has to answer a customer’s need. An agreement has to describe something the company can deliver. Your team needs enough understanding and support to make it happen.

What I can own

Agree on what I will own.

01

Product and commercial direction

I work with you on customer discovery, product strategy, packaging, pricing, and enterprise sales. We shape a roadmap around what customers value and what your company needs to earn.

02

Technology that supports your plans

I can take responsibility for architecture, data, integrations, vendor choices, and AI workflows. We consider the cost of building and running the product, and what the team will need to support it.

03

A team able to deliver

I help shape the team, hire where needed, and make ownership clear. At One to One Health I hired the founding engineers; at BCBST I led 22 direct reports and worked with a separate matrix of more than 130 people.

Working together

Agree on what the role needs to accomplish.

We begin with what you want to achieve, the team already in place, and the decisions ahead. A title alone will not tell us what the company needs from the role. I want to understand what would be different if the engagement worked well.

We then agree on the deliverables, decision authority, time commitment, team support, and measures. We may agree on a focused project, an advisory role, or ongoing product and technology leadership. We settle that scope before I take it on.

I bring preparation, curiosity, and a willingness to lead. I also want your team to have room to question the plan and improve it as we learn.
