Product & technology leadership

A shared direction.
Someone to lead
the work.

I work directly with founders and operating teams on the choices that connect customers, product, technology, and revenue. Together, we define the result and the responsibility needed to deliver it.

Who this serves

Founders, operating teams, and portfolio companies.

Bring a new product, a hard growth decision, or a delivery problem that needs business and technical judgment in the same conversation.

What I can own

The decisions around
the product and the business.

01

Product and market

Customer discovery, product strategy, packaging, pricing, and the path to an enterprise buyer. Make the commercial purpose clear before the roadmap fills up.

02

Technology and AI

Architecture, data, integrations, vendor choices, and AI workflows. Connect technical decisions to the work the company needs to do.

03

Teams and delivery

Team shape, hiring, clear ownership, and the plan for delivery. Make decisions and progress visible so people can contribute and follow through.

How the engagement works

We agree on the role
before I take it on.

Start with the business need

We talk through the goal, current team, decisions, constraints, and work already underway. Questions help us uncover what the role really needs to accomplish.

Make ownership explicit

We agree on the deliverables, decision authority, time commitment, team support, and measures. A focused project, advisory role, or ongoing leadership engagement can follow from that scope.

Start a conversation

Tell me about the company
and the decision ahead.

I’m interested in the people, the challenge, and where my experience can help.
