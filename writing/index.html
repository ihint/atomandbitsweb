Guides & writing

Questions I keep asking, written down.

Most of these pieces began as a question in a meeting that changed what a team decided to build. They come from the places I know best: founding and selling TheraMatch, leading digital products inside a health plan, running provider networks, and building software alongside the people who use it.

If you’re building or leading a company, start with the essays on payers, automation, access, and choosing technology leadership. If you run a practice and are weighing insurance, the two guides further down test that decision with your own numbers.

For founders and operators

Building healthcare products that hold up in operations.

Essays on selling to payers, automating claims without inventing certainty, turning a match into care, and choosing who should own your technology decisions.

GUIDE

How to choose a healthcare CTO and product partner

Compare advisors, agencies, fractional CTOs, and strategy partners by what each will own, the evidence behind them, and the outcome you receive.

INSIGHT

What healthtech founders underestimate about payers

Five operating realities to understand before you sell to or integrate with a health plan, from benefit design to truthful transaction states.

INSIGHT

Why automated claims need explicit control states

Prepared, submitted, accepted, and paid are different facts. Keep state, authority, approvals, retries, and evidence explicit before you automate.

INSIGHT

From clinical matching to a behavioral health operating system

A good match only starts access. Provider data, navigation, network participation, and payment decide whether a recommendation becomes care.

METHOD

Healthcare operating systems: from payer to payment

How I design healthcare products around the outcome, workflow, authority, transaction, and evidence that make a result dependable.

For practices

Deciding whether to accept insurance.

Both guides help an independent practice test the decision with its own numbers before changing how it gets paid. When you’re ready to try one lane, Claims Native is the product I’m building for that work.

GUIDE

Should your cash-pay practice accept insurance?

Test measured demand, conservative economics, operating fit, and a safe way back out before you change how your practice gets paid.

GUIDE

Can you accept insurance without hiring a biller?

Map every insurance queue to an owner, a stop rule, proof of completion, and backup coverage before you decide a new hire is unnecessary.
