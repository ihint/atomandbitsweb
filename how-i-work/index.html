How I work

I take the work seriously.
And enjoy the people
I get to do it with.

Curiosity is a large part of what makes this work a joy for me. I like learning how a business works, hearing what people see from their part of it, and thinking together about what would make it better.

Before the conversation

I want to understand before I offer an answer.

I put a lot of time into research before I form a response. I want to know how the business makes money, what people are trying to accomplish, and where the product or technology gets in the way.

That preparation gives me a point of view to bring to the conversation. I also want to hear what I’ve missed. The people doing the work know things I won’t find in a document, and a good question gives them room to share those things.

A conversation at TPN

Sometimes the question changes what we decide to build.

At TPN, we were talking about invoicing payers directly. I wanted to understand what we were trying to achieve: throughput, invoicing, payouts? Naming the goal mattered.

“What exactly are we trying to achieve here?”

That question helped us work out that we needed an internal claims filing system and a platform to manage revenue capture. It changed the scope of what we were building.

This is why I care so much about language. I want us to understand what we mean, have room to question it, and reach an agreement that everyone can explain.

Thinking and doing

Once we agree on the direction, I’m ready to lead.

I enjoy thinking through a problem, and I’m comfortable taking responsibility for what follows. I can work through the business case, make product and technical decisions, build the team, and get involved in the build myself.

We should all understand what we’re creating, why it matters, and how we’ll know it helped. I want people to see their part in the result and feel able to contribute as we learn more.

That judgment includes knowing what to ask of the technology. At One to One Health, AI prepared a clinical note for the provider to review. At TPN, contracted claim repricing used fixed rules. In the agent workspaces I’m building now, people can review the sources and drafts before deciding what happens next.

The people I work with

Kindness and careful thought mean a great deal to me.

I most enjoy working with curious, kind, thoughtful people. I value a shared interest in learning and a willingness to consider what the next right thing is, especially when we have an opportunity to create value for a business and the people it serves.

There’s a lot to enjoy in that kind of company. Good questions, thoughtful answers, and the chance to build something useful together.
