How I work

Questions first.
A shared deliverable.
Then the work.

I approach hard problems with joy and curiosity. I ask questions to gather what I’m missing, understand other perspectives, and find the change that matters to the business.

Working together

We should mean the same thing by “done.”

Before we build, we agree on the problem, the deliverable, who it serves, and how we’ll judge the result.

From conversation to delivery

I bring the business, product,
and technology into one view.

01

Ask and listen

I want to understand the business, the people doing the work, and what they have already tried. Questions give us new information and a chance to challenge our assumptions.

02

Shape it together

I reflect back the problem, invite correction, and work with you on the deliverable. Shared understanding and buy-in give the work a clear purpose.

03

Build and learn

I lead the agreed work, make progress and decisions visible, and test the result. We revisit the plan when the evidence changes.

Judgment in practice

Different work calls
for different tools.

01

Use AI where interpretation helps.

In the One to One Health SOAP-note workflow, AI prepared a draft from the encounter information. The provider reviewed it before it entered the chart.

Read the clinical AI case ↗
02

Use rules where the answer must be exact.

At TPN, my work connected payer terms, claims, repricing, and payment. A model’s suggestion and a financial transaction needed different controls.

Read the claims and payment case ↗
03

Keep people involved where their judgment matters.

In the agent workspaces I’m building, sources and drafts sit beside the next decision. The person can review the work and decide what may happen next.

What we agree on

A deliverable your team
can understand and use.

01

The change

What needs to be different for the customer or the business? What do we know, and what still needs to be learned?

02

The responsibility

What will I own? What does your team own? Which decisions need to be made together?

03

The evidence

What will show that the work helped? We choose measures for time, cost, quality, adoption, or revenue before we begin.

Start with the question

What do we need
to understand together?

You can bring an unfinished idea or a hard operating problem. We’ll work out the useful next question.
