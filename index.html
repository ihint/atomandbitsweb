02 / Curiositya&b

There’s a person
on the other side
of the product.

That’s where I like to begin.

For 15 years, I’ve worked on systems people depend on and rarely see.

At PerfectServe, that meant helping a nurse reach a doctor at 2 a.m. At BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee, it meant helping a member get more from their health benefits. At One to One Health, it meant cutting charting time by about 20%. At TheraMatch, it meant helping someone find the right therapist.

Every new workflow still piques my curiosity about who it serves and what it could become. And most importantly, how we can make it simpler for people to actually get care.

That’s what draws me to product: the people, the questions, and the joy of building something useful together.

03 / Product judgmentMake the first use count

The first hour
is part of
the product.

At PerfectServe, new providers went from waiting five to seven days to starting in under an hour.

I brought what hospital leaders and clinicians needed into product decisions. As PerfectServe’s first product manager, I helped build the product and admin systems that made that change possible.

What does each new customer need before they can benefit from what they bought? The answer can change the product, how we introduce it, or the support around it. I love figuring that out with the people who know the job.
