One to One Health

Scaling care delivery

When I joined One to One Health, patients valued the care and clinicians enjoyed delivering it. But each new client asked more of a team already relying on manual processes, spreadsheets, and knowledge held by a few people.

Eligibility and onboarding belonged in our product plan alongside clinical care. I set that direction for the full product suite and hired the founding engineers. We also needed to give clinical and operations staff something useful before that wider plan could deliver.

I chose clinical documentation as a place to start. I designed and developed the initial AI infrastructure to prepare clinical notes for provider review. Charting time fell from 22 to 17.5 minutes across all encounters, while clinician and patient satisfaction were maintained or improved.

BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee↗

Optimizing self service.

Making it easier for members to help themselves also made the health plan less costly to serve. I led digital products serving about 3.5 million members and built the teams behind them. More people used the digital services, while self-service reduced administrative costs.

Digital adoption more than doubled · $500K+ reduction in admin costs
