From a first customer to millions of people.

TheraMatch / TPN Match

Finding a therapist could leave someone with a list of names and another difficult decision. I co-founded TheraMatch to help people make that first connection, built the matching product and early clinical framework, and led its sale to TPN.health, where it became TPN Match.

Acquired by TPN.health in nine months, then scaled to $1M+ ARR and 1M members.

BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee

Making it easier for members to help themselves also made the health plan less costly to serve. I led digital products serving about 3.5 million members and built the teams behind them. More people used the digital services, while self-service reduced administrative costs.

3.5M members, NPS above 60, digital adoption more than doubled.

PerfectServe

Selling to a hospital means making a promise that has to hold up through rollout and daily use. At PerfectServe, I connected hospital implementations with product and commercial decisions, including how to bring the product into new markets.

$2M to $40M ARR · 10 health-system rollouts.

Paragon Truss

At Paragon Truss, I led the company’s first enterprise deal, including the contracts and client relationship. That meant connecting what the customer needed with what Paragon could commit to delivering.

$500K a year, the largest contract in company history.

One to One Health
