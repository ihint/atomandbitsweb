Practice billing guide · Reviewed October 15, 2026

Can you use a billing service and keep your EHR?

Yes. You can keep your existing EHR when you choose billing support. The important question is how visits leave that system and how payments get back into it. We confirm the connection approach during onboarding.

Prepare a billing question

Review an email draft before you send it. No account or estimate needed. Leave out patient and bank details.

Start with one recent week

Ask your EHR vendor which visit or charge reports your office can export. Start with a sample that has patient details removed. Check whether the file carries the service dates, codes and amounts needed to assess the billing flow. Do not send patient records through a marketing form or email.

A file export, a dedicated user login and a direct connection through an EHR’s interface are three different approaches. One does not prove that another exists. The right choice depends on your system, access rules and the scope you agree to.

Check the return path too

Before choosing a billing service, ask who posts payments, where adjustments appear and how the office can inspect a correction. A claim sent from an export does not by itself keep the EHR’s balances up to date. Agree who owns that step and how you will check it.

  • Can the practice limit and revoke the service’s access?
  • Does the EHR permit the proposed use under its terms?
  • Who approves a change that needs clinical judgment?
  • What happens when an export is missing a field or a posting fails?

What Claims Native can confirm

Our setup starts with your specialty, payers and current EHR. A visit file is the starting point; we review the layout with you. A dedicated Claims Native login requires a review of your EHR and its terms. No direct EHR connection is live yet. Your EHR name alone does not establish a ready connection.

We agree the work and price and sign the agreement and business associate agreement before patient records reach us. Your provider keeps clinical and coding authority. Your practice approves the switch after the shadow period.

What to bring to a first conversation

Bring your EHR name, the kind of practice you run, your main payers and the billing step you want help with. Leave patient details out. Read how setup works, or check which enrollments need to be ready.
