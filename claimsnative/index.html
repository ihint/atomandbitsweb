Questions before you accept insurance

Know what you are taking on.

The review is meant to make the revenue, cost, work, and limits clear before you change how the practice operates.

Can a small practice accept insurance without hiring a biller?

Yes. Claims Native gives a cash-pay practice one fully autonomous AI front office to own insurance operations without requiring the practice to hire and manage a biller.

What work does accepting insurance add to a practice?

The work may include payer readiness, eligibility, claim preparation, submission, rejection and denial follow-up, remittance review, payment matching, patient balances, and posting.

Does Claims Native handle billing and reconciliation?

Claims Native serves as the AI front office. It coordinates payer setup, claim work, follow-up, denials, payment evidence, and reconciliation. When judgment is needed, it routes the exception behind the scenes and keeps your practice focused on the decision.

Does Claims Native guarantee more revenue or reimbursement?

No. The calculator is a planning estimate. Coverage, payer rules, denials, patient cost share, collection rates, displaced cash revenue, fees, and practice labor all affect the result.

What does the 90-day pilot test?

It tests one narrow claim path with real practice data. The practice measures collected revenue, office time, fees, and unresolved work before it decides whether to expand.
