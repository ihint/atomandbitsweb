Four common questions

Know what the first test can prove.

The pilot starts with claim preparation. It does not present an unproven end-to-end insurance operation as live.

What does Claims Native do now?

Claims Native turns visit exports into a short approval queue, applies practice rules, and prepares the next claim package. Office staff and providers review the required decisions and keep control.

Is electronic claim submission live?

Electronic claim work stays in dry-run mode until live evidence proves the route and the practice enables it.

What does the 90-day pilot cost and test?

The pilot costs $500 for the guided launch and $249 per month for 90 days. It tests one location and one narrow path from visit export through practice rules, approvals, and the next claim package. It includes 200 visits per month; additional visits are $1.25 each.

Does Claims Native guarantee revenue or reimbursement?

No. The calculator is a planning estimate, not a revenue promise. The pilot does not guarantee submission, payer acceptance, reimbursement, revenue, or legal correctness.
