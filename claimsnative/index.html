What Claims Native does

The whole billing job, from the signed note to the bank.

Most practices meet Claims Native the day a signed visit becomes a claim. Behind that, we check coverage, scrub against payer rules, file through the clearinghouse, chase what goes quiet, post what comes back and match it to your deposits.

Check · File · Follow · Post · Match

  • Visit to claim

    Every signed visit in your EHR becomes a claim. Nothing gets retyped.

  • Coverage check

    Eligibility verified before filing, so a coverage problem shows up today, not on a denial in six weeks.

  • Claim scrub

    Each claim checked against that payer's rules. We fix what we can and ask you only for what we can't.

  • Filing

    Out through the clearinghouse, with the acknowledgment kept at the claim level.

  • Follow-up

    Every filing deadline tracked. Every claim that goes quiet gets chased.

  • Posting

    Each payment posted into your EHR, with every adjustment preserved.

  • Patient balance

    What the patient owes, worked out from the payer's payment and explanation, never guessed.

  • Deposit match

    Every bank deposit matched to the payment behind it. "They paid" and "it's in your account" are two different dates, and we track both.

What you get back: paid claims, and a short list of what needs you.

Why Claims Native

Most billers send you a report. We leave you a record.

A report tells you what happened last month. A record shows you every claim, every version, every response and every dollar, the day it happens.

  • Your EHR stays the record.

    Reads it. Posts back into it. No new system to learn.

    Visits come from your EHR. Claims go out through the clearinghouse. Payments post back with the adjustment. Your money keeps going to your bank. We never hold it.

  • Short payments stay open.

    A short payment is a question, not an answer.

    We check the difference against the payer's explanation and your contract. When the contract supports more, we go back for it: corrected claim, reconsideration or appeal. Then we record what that payer needed, so the next claim goes out the way they want it.

  • Three things we never touch.

    Your judgment. Your collections. Your intake.

    Your provider decides what the signed note supports. Collecting patient balances stays with your office. Wrong insurance on file or no coverage on the date of service: we report it, you own it.

  • Founder-led.

    You talk to the person who does the work.

    Ian Harman built the software and runs the service from Chattanooga, Tennessee. BAA signed before any patient record reaches us. Your records stay yours and leave with you.

How we start
