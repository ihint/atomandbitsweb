Questions

Will my claims be accurate?

Every claim is checked against your notes and your payer's rules before it goes out. If something does not match, it stops and tells you why.

Is my data secure?

Your data stays in your practice's own workspace. Nothing goes out without a named person's approval, and nothing is shared with an AI model unless you turn that on.

What does it cost?

$500 to start, then $249 a month for the 90-day pilot. 200 visits a month are included; more are $1.25 each.

Does this guarantee I get paid?

No. Payers decide what they pay and when. Claims Native makes sure every claim goes out right, follows up before deadlines, and posts every payment to the cent.
