Questions before you accept insurance

Know what you are taking on.

The review is meant to make the revenue, cost, work, and limits clear before you change how the practice operates.

Can a small practice add insurance without hiring a biller?

Claims Native helps independent outpatient practices add insurance without adding a billing department. Office staff and providers still review decisions and keep control.

What does Claims Native do today?

It turns visit exports into a short approval queue, applies practice rules, and prepares the next claim artifact. It does not claim to handle payer setup, eligibility, live submission, follow-up, denials, payment, or posting today.

Is the electronic claim path live?

Electronic claim work stays in dry-run mode until live evidence proves the route and the practice enables it. Your named office and clinical reviewers approve the required decisions.

What is the future direction?

The category direction is post-visit insurance revenue operations. The earned promise, not a current capability, is a completed visit carried through payer response, payment, and practice-system posting only after each stage has live proof.

Does Claims Native guarantee more revenue or reimbursement?

No. The calculator is a planning estimate, not a revenue promise. The pilot does not guarantee submission, payer acceptance, reimbursement, revenue, or legal correctness.

What does the 90-day pilot test?

It tests one narrow path from visit export through practice rules, approvals, and the next claim artifact. The practice measures office time, fees, and unresolved work before it decides whether to expand.
