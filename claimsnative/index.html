A few useful answers

Clear before
you get started.

Will I need to manage every claim?

The aim is to let routine billing continue after you agree the setup. You can choose to review each claim first. Clinical questions and changes to care always come back to your provider.

What happens when a payment is short?

Claims Native keeps the difference open for investigation. We check the payer’s explanation and the applicable contract before deciding whether more is owed. A difference does not become a patient bill by default.

Can I keep Jane or my current EHR?

Keeping your care records in your EHR is the starting point. We first check whether its treatment export contains the details the agreed billing scope needs. The current walkthrough uses a supplied sample file; a live Jane or other EHR connection is not being promised.

Can I use this for live billing today?

We’re starting with scoped chiropractic work in Chattanooga. The current workspace is a preview. Before real billing, we agree the scope and price and verify source access, practice permissions, payer participation and payment routes.
