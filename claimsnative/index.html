One day in, one queue out

The system does the claims work. Your team keeps control.

Claims Native handles routing, checking, preparation, and follow-up inside the practice rules you set. People are asked only for judgment the system cannot safely make.

01 · Import

Hand over the day

Upload a Jane CSV. Every row is validated, and bad rows stay visible instead of disappearing.

02 · Prepare

Safe work happens

Agents load practice context, scrub the claim, apply payer rules, and prepare the next artifact.

03 · Decide

Approve with evidence

The office manager and provider see what changed, why it stopped, and what approval allows next.

04 · Release

Get a tangible result

After both approvals, release a printable superbill package or an unmistakable electronic dry run.

Trust is the product

Nothing consequential happens quietly.

Every correction, rule, approval, and release is recorded. A claim cannot leave before the practice’s required approvals are present.

Nothing leaves without you.

Office approval + provider approval + unchanged claim version

See the evidence

Source row, service lines, diagnoses, fees, agent corrections, payer rules, and practice SOPs stay beside the recommendation.

Know what is not real

A dry run is labeled as a dry run. Prepared, released, submitted, accepted, denied, and paid remain different states.
