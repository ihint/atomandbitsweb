The sequence

Seven steps, in order.

  1. You create an account, or talk to me first.

    Find your practice by its NPI and tell us which EHR you use. If you'd rather talk first, 20 minutes covers your EHR, the payers you bill and who does your billing today.

  2. One export of a recent week.

    Send one export of a recent week from your EHR, with patient details removed. We map your columns from it and show you what we read, so you can tell us where we got it wrong before anything real depends on it.

  3. The agreement and the business associate agreement.

    You sign both before any patient record reaches us. Your records stay yours.

  4. We connect your EHR export and capture your contract rates.

    We set up the visit export from your EHR. We record your payer contract rates from your contracts and fee schedules, and we check each rate against its source. This is the number every short payment gets measured against later, so we get it from the document, not from memory.

  5. Enrollment with the clearinghouse and your payers.

    This takes weeks. The payer sets the pace. We chase it and tell you where it stands.

  6. A shadow period.

    We prepare and check every claim beside your current biller. Each week you see the comparison: what we found, what they filed, where the two differ. If we're not better, you'll see that too.

  7. Live filing.

    Your office and your provider approve the switch. Then we file, follow up and post.

What we need from you

Four things.

  • One named office contact. The person we ask when a claim needs a fact only your office has.
  • One provider. The person who answers a question about a signed note and approves the switch to live filing.
  • An EHR billing user. A login in your EHR for Claims Native, so payments post back into your records.
  • Your payer contracts. The contracts and fee schedules we check each payment against.

Before you sign

Where things stand.

Four things I'd rather you read now than discover later.

  • The shadow period runs on your own visits while enrollment completes. Nothing goes to a payer until your office and your provider approve live filing.
  • ChiroHD and Jane are the EHRs we take visits from today. Other systems wait on a sample export from a practice.
  • Nothing on this page promises a payer's timing or a payer's payment.
  • Clinical, coding, financial and release authority stay with the practice.
