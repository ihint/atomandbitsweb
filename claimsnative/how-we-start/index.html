How we start

From signing up to live filing.
In the order it happens. Your current biller keeps working until the comparison says we are ready.

Seven steps. Two of them wait on outside parties: the payers set the pace of enrollment, and your office and your provider approve the switch to live filing.

The sequence

Seven steps, in order.

  1. You create an account, or talk to us first.

    Find your practice by its NPI and tell us which EHR you use. If you would rather talk first, 20 minutes covers your EHR, the payers you bill and who does your billing today.

  2. One export of a recent week.

    Send one export of a recent week from your EHR, with patient details removed. We map your columns from it and show you what we read.

  3. The agreement and the business associate agreement.

    You sign both before any patient record reaches us. Your records stay yours.

  4. We connect your EHR export and capture your contract rates.

    We set up the visit export from your EHR. We record your payer contract rates from your contracts and fee schedules, and we check each rate against its source.

  5. Enrollment with the clearinghouse and your payers.

    This takes weeks. The payer sets the pace. We chase it and tell you where it stands.

  6. A shadow period.

    We prepare and check every claim beside your current biller. Each week you see the comparison: what we found, what they filed, where the two differ.

  7. Live filing.

    Your office and your provider approve the switch. Then we file, follow up and post.

What we need from you

Four things.

  • One named office contact. The person we ask when a claim needs a fact only your office has.
  • One provider. The person who answers a question about a signed note and approves the switch to live filing.
  • An EHR billing user. A login in your EHR for Claims Native, so payments post back into your records.
  • Your payer contracts. The contracts and fee schedules we check each payment against.

What we do

  • The night before each clinic day. We check insurance for every insured patient on the next day’s schedule. By morning your front desk has one sheet: active or not, copay, deductible left, visits used and left, and anything that needs a call.
  • Every week. We check status on every open claim with the payer. Anything older than 30 days gets worked: we find out why it is stuck and what will fix it.
  • When a claim is denied or stuck. Fixes go to your existing biller. We hand over what to fix, with a drafted corrected claim or appeal. A person reviews every draft. Your biller or your office submits it. Nothing goes to a payer without your OK.
  • Every Friday. A one-page money report: money expected, money stuck, what we did this week, and the few items that need you.

Who it is for

  • You have a biller or billing staff you trust, and you do not want to switch.
  • Claims still come back denied because coverage was inactive, visits were used up, or the plan changed since the last check.
  • Claims sit at 30, 60, or 90 days and nobody knows why. One chiropractor told us his staff would wait 1.5 hours on hold, then write the claim off.
  • Your front desk spends hours on payer portals checking benefits by hand.

Goals we work toward

These are goals, not promises. We measure them from week one and report them every Friday. Nothing here promises a payer’s timing or a payer’s payment.

  • Every insured patient on the schedule checked before the visit.
  • No surprise inactive-coverage denials.
  • Every claim over 30 days has a known status and a next step.
  • Fewer write-offs, and hours back for your front desk each week.
