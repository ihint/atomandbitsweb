Questions

What happens on day one?

We connect your visit export, set up your payer list and clearinghouse, and prepare the enrollment forms for your signature. Your first claims run that week.

When do claims reach my payers?

As soon as each payer's enrollment completes and you say go. Until then every claim is still checked, prepared, and ready, so nothing is lost while you wait.

What do I have to do each week?

Read one short list each morning and approve the few items that need you. Join a twenty-minute review once a week.

What does it cost?

$500 to start, then $249 a month for 90 days. 200 visits a month are included; more are $1.25 each.

Does this guarantee I get paid?

No. Payers decide what they pay and when. Claims Native makes sure every claim goes out right, follows up before deadlines, and posts every payment to the cent.

What happens after 90 days?

We sit down with your numbers: what went out, what came in, what needed you. You decide whether to keep going, change something, or stop.
