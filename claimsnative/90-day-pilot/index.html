90-day outline

Connect, run in test mode, then go live.

Launch · before week 1

Connect and enroll

  • Confirm the location, the export, the two approvers, and the practice rules.
  • Start payer and clearinghouse enrollment; we prepare the forms, you sign.
  • The $500 guided launch covers this.

Weeks 1 to 2

Every lane in test mode

  • Your visits run through all thirteen lanes.
  • Filing goes to the test clearinghouse; acknowledgments come back and are kept.
  • You read the ledger and tune the rules.

Weeks 3 to 13

Filing live, weekly review

  • When enrollment completes and you turn it on, filing goes to your payers.
  • Denials, 835 posting, deposit matching, and patient balances run as they arrive.
  • Each week: what ran, what stopped, who acted, what is due.

Enrollment follows each payer's timing. If a payer is not enrolled by week three, that lane stays in test mode for that payer and the weekly record says so.

Questions

Know what the 90 days cover.

What does the pilot run?

Every lane of the department on your own visits: coverage, authorization, coding review, scrub and correction, release, filing and acknowledgments, status follow-up, denials and appeals, 835 posting, deposit matching, patient balances, and a daily close.

What does it cost?

$500 guided launch, then $249 per month for 90 days. The monthly fee includes 200 visits; each additional visit is $1.25.

When does filing go live?

When your payer and clearinghouse enrollment completes and you turn it on. Until then every lane runs and filing goes to the test clearinghouse. The page always says which mode it is in.

Does a filed claim mean the payer accepted it?

No. Filing produces an acknowledgment. Acceptance, adjudication, and payment are the payer's, and each arrives as its own evidence on the ledger.

Who decides what?

Agents do the routine work. A person acts for a legal act, a clinical judgment, a financial authority above your limit, or missing evidence. Each stop names who.

What happens after 90 days?

We review the ledger with you: what ran, what stopped, practice time, and every payer result backed by source evidence. You decide whether to stop, adjust, or expand.
