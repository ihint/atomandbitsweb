A small start

See it. Agree it.
Then we start.

  1. See the app.

    Open the app and find out whether the next action is clear without learning a new billing system.

  2. Agree what we’ll handle.

    Name the supported payer, services, work and price. Clinical decisions stay with your provider.

  3. Prove the value in your practice.

    Before live work, we verify data access, permissions and payer routes. Then we measure office time, collections and unresolved balances together.

What belongs in the service?

Claim corrections, payer follow-up, payment investigation and insurance reconciliation are the core service. They stay with us.

Bookkeeping across your systems, monthly close and older-balance cleanup are separate requests. You approve the work and price first.

Live billing starts after we agree.

Creating an account does not enroll your practice, start live billing or commit you to a paid service. It lets you look around and tell us what you need.
