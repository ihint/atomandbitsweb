A small start

See it. Agree it.
Then try it.

  1. See the workspace.

    Walk through the workspace. Find out whether the next action is clear without learning a new billing system.

  2. Agree what we’ll handle.

    Name the supported payer, services, work and price. Clinical decisions stay with your provider.

  3. Prove the value in your practice.

    Before real work, verify the data access, permissions and payer routes. Then measure office time, collections and unresolved balances.

What belongs in the service?

Routine claim corrections, payer follow-up and insurance payment investigation belong in the agreed core service.

Bookkeeping across your systems, monthly close and older-balance cleanup are separate requests. You approve the work and price first.
