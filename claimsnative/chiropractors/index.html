Where we start

BCBS commercial plans first.

We take on your BCBS commercial claims first. Other payers and secondary claims follow, one payer at a time, once their rules pass our checks.

We check the patient’s coverage before we file. A coverage problem shows up before the claim goes out, not weeks later on a denial.

Coding review

Each CMT claim checked against the signed note.

Codes 98940 to 98942. We read the note. We never change it.

  • The AT modifier. On each CMT line the note supports as active treatment.
  • The regions match the code. 98940, 98941 or 98942 matches the number of spinal regions in the signed note.
  • The M99 segmental dysfunction code first. One for each region adjusted, listed before anything else.
  • Symptom codes second. Pain and other symptom codes follow the M99 codes.
  • The initial treatment date on the note. We read it from the signed note, where the payer expects it.

When the note does not support the claim, the question goes to your provider, once. Your provider decides what the note says.

Then the rest

Filed, followed, posted, matched.

  1. Filing. Each claim goes out through the clearinghouse.
  2. Follow-up. We track every filing deadline and every claim that goes quiet.
  3. Posting. We post each payment into your EHR and keep every adjustment.
  4. Patient balance. We work out what the patient owes from the payer’s payment and explanation.
  5. Deposit match. We match each bank deposit to the payment behind it.

What we do

  • The night before each clinic day. We check insurance for every insured patient on the next day’s schedule. By morning your front desk has one sheet: active or not, copay, deductible left, visits used and left, and anything that needs a call.
  • Every week. We check status on every open claim with the payer. Anything older than 30 days gets worked: we find out why it is stuck and what will fix it.
  • When a claim is denied or stuck. Fixes go to your existing biller. We hand over what to fix, with a drafted corrected claim or appeal. A person reviews every draft. Your biller or your office submits it. Nothing goes to a payer without your OK.
  • Every Friday. A one-page money report: money expected, money stuck, what we did this week, and the few items that need you.

Who it is for

  • You have a biller or billing staff you trust, and you do not want to switch.
