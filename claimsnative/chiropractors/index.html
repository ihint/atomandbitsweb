Coding review

Each CMT claim checked against the signed note.

Codes 98940 to 98942. We read the note. We never change it. Most chiropractic denials I've seen come from one of five places, so those are the five we check on every claim:

  • The AT modifier. On each CMT line the note supports as active treatment.
  • The regions match the code. 98940, 98941 or 98942 matches the number of spinal regions in the signed note.
  • The M99 segmental dysfunction code first. One for each region adjusted, listed before anything else.
  • Symptom codes second. Pain and other symptom codes follow the M99 codes.
  • The initial treatment date on the note. We read it from the signed note, where the payer expects it.

When the note doesn't support the claim, the question goes to your provider, once. Your provider decides what the note says. We don't.

Then the rest

Filed, followed, posted, matched.

  1. Filing. Each claim goes out through the clearinghouse.
  2. Follow-up. We track every filing deadline and every claim that goes quiet.
  3. Posting. We post each payment into your EHR and keep every adjustment.
  4. Patient balance. We work out what the patient owes from the payer's payment and explanation.
  5. Deposit match. We match each bank deposit to the payment behind it.

Questions

Questions practices ask.

What is Claims Native?

Claims Native is a billing service for an independent practice. We check coverage, file every claim, chase every payer and post every payment back into your EHR. Your money keeps going to your bank. We never hold it.

Who is it for?

Full billing is for a practice billing up to $10,000 a month in insurance charges. A larger practice gets a quote from its volume. Claims Native is built for practices without a billing department, across medical specialties: chiropractic practices, cash-pay practices adding insurance, and practices that keep their current biller.

Which specialties do you work with?

Practices across medical specialties that bill professional claims to insurers. Primary care, behavioral health, cardiology, dermatology, orthopedics and chiropractic are examples, not a full list. We do not bill hospital or facility claims. Tell us your specialty, and we look at your codes and payers during setup.

Where do you operate?

Nationwide. We work with practices across the U.S. from Chattanooga, Tennessee, and file claims through a national clearinghouse. That does not mean every payer is ready on day one. We add payers one at a time and confirm yours during setup. Secondary claims come later.

What does it cost?

Full billing is $500 a month for a practice billing up to $10,000 a month in insurance charges. A larger practice gets a quote from its volume. One launch fee, quoted with your scope, covers enrollment, connecting your EHR, capturing your contract rates, and the shadow period. Claim corrections, payer follow-up and appeals, payment investigation, insurance reconciliation, posting into your EHR, and fixing our own errors are included. Bookkeeping, monthly close, cleanup of older balances, and patient collections are priced separately. After the launch period it runs month to month.

Starter is $990 a month per location, for a practice that keeps its current biller. It covers insurance checks before each clinic day and follow-up on unpaid claims, beside the billing you already have. Nothing about your current biller changes until you decide it should.

Do I have to switch my EHR or my biller?

No. Keep your existing EHR. We'll confirm the connection approach during onboarding: a visit file, which any practice can send today, or a Claims Native login in your EHR, which we set up with you. We post payments back into your EHR. There is no new system to learn.

You do not have to leave your biller to start. On full billing, your current biller keeps working through a shadow period, and live filing starts when your office and your provider approve it. On Starter, nothing about your current biller changes until you decide it should.

Which payers and EHRs does it work with today?

Any practice can send visits as a file today. A Claims Native login in your EHR is set up with you during onboarding, starting with Jane and ChiroHD. No direct EHR connection is live yet. We bill BCBS commercial plans first. Medicare, delegated networks, and secondary claims come later, payer by payer.

What happens if my EHR isn't listed?

Choose "Another EHR" when you set up your practice, or name it in an email to us. We start from a visit file and review your system with you. A direct connection to a new system is development work, and we tell you so before you rely on one.

Do I sign a business associate agreement?

Yes. You sign the agreement and the business associate agreement before any patient record reaches us. Your records stay yours and leave with you.

How long does it take to go live?

Enrollment with the clearinghouse and your payers takes weeks, and the payer sets the pace. New practices start with a shadow period on their own visits while that enrollment completes. Live filing starts when your office and your provider approve it.
