What it checks for you

Manipulation codes

98940, 98941, and 98942 are matched to the spinal regions in your signed note. A mismatch stops and shows you both.

X-rays

Body area and views on the claim are matched to the order and the report before the claim goes out.

Coverage

Visit limits and plan rules are checked before the visit closes, so you know where the patient stands.

Maintenance-care denials

Caught the day they arrive, sorted by reason, and appealed with your signature and the note attached.

Authorizations

When a plan needs one, the request is prepared from your note. You sign it.

Payments

Every remittance posted from the payer's numbers. Patient share kept separate. Deposits matched to the cent.

Who decides what

  1. 1

    Claims Native

    Reads the visit, checks coverage, checks the code against the note, files, follows up, posts, matches.

  2. 2

    Your front desk

    Confirms patient and coverage facts when something does not match. One short list each morning.

  3. 3

    You

    Decide the clinical calls: the code, the diagnosis, the appeal. Everything else is done before you look.

Questions

Do you understand chiropractic billing?

Yes. Spinal manipulation codes are matched to the regions in your note, x-ray lines to the report, and maintenance-care denials are caught and appealed with your signature.

Who makes the clinical calls?

You do. If a code and a note do not agree, the claim stops and shows you both. Nothing picks a code for you.

Will it work with my system?

If your system can export visits, it works. Jane and ChiroTouch exports are set up already.
