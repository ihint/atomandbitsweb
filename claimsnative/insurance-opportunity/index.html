A planning estimate, not a revenue promise.

This is gross potential revenue, not net income or expected collections. It assumes each turned-away patient completes the full course of care at the value you entered. The estimate does not account for:

  • Payer enrollment, network status, or contract dates
  • Coverage, eligibility, or service limits
  • Allowed amounts and payer fee schedules
  • Claim rejections, denials, or payer adjustments
  • Patient cost share or uncollected balances
  • Billing, software, setup, or staffing costs
  • Practice capacity or clinical fit
  • Cash-pay visits displaced by insured visits
