What is real today
  • These guides explain how insurance billing works in general. Each payer’s published policy and your contract decide the specifics.
  • Nothing here is legal or coding advice. Your provider decides what the signed note supports.
  • Claims Native bills BCBS commercial plans first, from visits in ChiroHD and Jane. Medicare, delegated networks and secondary claims come later, payer by payer.
