    - paragraph: Practice billing guide · Reviewed October 15, 2026
    - heading "What needs to be ready before a billing service starts?" [level=1]
    - paragraph: A billing service needs an agreed scope, access to the right records, the required payer connections and a checked handoff from your current biller. Opening an account alone does not make a practice ready for live claims.
    - heading "Separate the enrollment steps" [level=2]
    - paragraph:
      - text: Credentialing checks a provider’s qualifications. Joining a payer’s network sets the participation relationship. Transaction enrollment sets up the exchange of electronic records through a clearinghouse. Completing one step does not complete the others.
      - link "Stedi explains these distinct processes":
        - /url: https://www.stedi.com/docs/healthcare/credentialing-and-enrollment
      - text: .
    - paragraph: Check the payer and the transaction together. The enrollment needed to send a professional claim may differ from the enrollment needed to receive its remittance. Confirm the required authorizations and effective status before depending on that route.
    - heading "Protect the current payment flow" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "An electronic remittance advice explains a payer’s claim decisions. Electronic funds transfer moves money. The two need separate checks. Changing where remittances arrive can affect your current biller: Stedi states that its ERA enrollment replaces an existing clearinghouse ERA route. Plan that handoff before requesting a change."
    - paragraph: Record who checks the old route, who checks the new route and how the practice will find a missing response. Confirm payment instructions with the payer and the practice’s authorized signer. Do not treat a portal connection or a submitted request as completed enrollment.
    - heading "Agree who does what" [level=2]
    - list:
      - listitem: Name the services, payers and work in scope, and the price.
      - listitem: Confirm how signed visits reach the billing service and how results return to your EHR.
      - listitem: Sign the service agreement and business associate agreement before sharing patient records.
      - listitem: Set the rules for provider questions, corrections and approvals.
      - listitem: Compare the work during a shadow period while your current biller keeps working.
    - heading "How Claims Native starts" [level=2]
    - paragraph: We review your practice’s EHR, specialty and payers during onboarding. We confirm the connection approach with you; no direct EHR connection is live yet. We add payers one at a time. Our scope is professional claims, and secondary claims come later. Enrollment status and practice approval remain separate from live filing.
