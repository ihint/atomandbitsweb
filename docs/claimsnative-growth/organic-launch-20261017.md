## Post text

Before you change billing companies, ask where each part of the handoff will go.

Which visits leave your EHR, and how?
Who follows a rejected claim?
Where does the payer send its payment report?
Who checks that report against the bank deposit?

A payment report explains the payer's decision. It does not prove the money reached your bank. And an unexplained difference needs investigation before it becomes a patient bill.

At Claims Native, we're working through these setup questions with the practice's existing EHR in mind. We confirm the access and payment paths before live billing starts.

If you own or run an independent practice and are weighing a billing change, which EHR do you use, and where does the handoff get stuck? Please keep patient details out of comments.
