Corridor Robotics

AI is coming to the factory floor. Let’s put it to work in Chattanooga first.

AI can now turn a product design into a full build plan, with stations, quality checks and work instructions, and hand assembly to robots. Someone still has to bring those tools inside a real plant, fit them to how your people work, and keep them running. That’s Corridor Robotics: a local team that finds where AI and automation will pay off, builds it with you, and stays to run it.

Tell me what you’d automate first

We make things here.

Chattanooga sits on one of the busiest manufacturing and freight corridors in the Southeast. Manufacturers here are short on people and long on work. The tools to close that gap are finally good enough, but most plants don’t have anyone whose job is to put them to use.

What we do

  1. Walk the floor. We spend time where the work happens and find the one or two tasks where automation will pay for itself first.
  2. Build around how your people work. Off-the-shelf robots and AI models from proven makers, fitted to your line, your systems and your team, not the other way around.
  3. Stay and run it. Monitoring, tuning and support after launch.

Where it fits

  • Palletizing, case handling and moving material around the plant
  • Quality checks and inspection
  • Scheduling, inventory and the spreadsheets that run the plant
  • Back-office work: orders, invoices, compliance paperwork

The same approach works for Chattanooga’s banks, insurers and health systems. Manufacturing is just where it starts.
